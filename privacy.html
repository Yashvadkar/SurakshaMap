Privacy Policy

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

SurakshaMap is designed from the ground up to protect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII). No names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device fingerprints are collected during the reporting process.

2. What We Collect

When you submit a report, we collect only:

  • Issue category (e.g., broken streetlight, pothole)
  • Description of the hazard
  • GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude)
  • Severity level
  • Optional photo evidence
  • Timestamp of submission

None of this data is linked to any identity.

3. Photo Handling

If you upload a photo, it is compressed and all EXIF metadata (which may contain location, device, and timestamp data) is stripped before storage. Photos are used solely for evidence of the reported hazard.

4. AI Processing

Report descriptions are sent to Google's Gemini AI for automated classification (genuine vs. spam detection). This processing involves only the text content of the report — no personal data is transmitted.

5. Tracking Tokens

Upon submission, you receive a random tracking token (e.g., SM-XXXXXX). This token is not linked to any identity and serves solely as a lookup key for report status. Tokens are stored locally in your browser.

6. Data Storage & Security

Report data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) with security rules that restrict write access. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Administrator access requires authentication.

7. Contact

For privacy concerns or data deletion requests, contact the project maintainers through the project's GitHub repository.
