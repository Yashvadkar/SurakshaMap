About SurakshaMap

Community-powered safety intelligence for Indian cities.

🎯 Our Mission

SurakshaMap is a privacy-preserving platform where citizens can report public space safety issues — broken streetlights, potholes, unsafe areas, waterlogging, and more — without creating an account or sharing personal information.

🛡️ Privacy First

We collect zero personally identifiable information. No names, no emails, no phone numbers. Reports are completely anonymous. Photo metadata (EXIF) is stripped before upload.

Read Privacy Policy →

🤖 AI-Powered Review

Reports are automatically reviewed by Google's Gemini AI to filter spam and detect duplicates before reaching city administrators, ensuring faster response to genuine issues.

⚙️ Technology

Firebase Leaflet Maps Chart.js Gemini AI Vanilla JS CSS Custom Properties

📊 Quality Expectations

  • ✅ Privacy-preserving anonymous reporting
  • ✅ Evidence-based safety analysis with explainable risk scoring
  • ✅ No individual profiling or automated accusations
  • ✅ Actionable intervention recommendations
  • ✅ Clear limitations and impact measurement
